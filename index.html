Manoj Datta

Ph.D. Student in Agricultural and Applied Economics

I study international trade policy and agricultural trade as a Ph.D. student at Virginia Tech, advised by Dr. Jason Grant. My research applies econometric methods, including gravity models, panel data methods, and spatial and event-study designs, to questions in trade policy and agricultural markets. Before Virginia Tech, I earned an M.S. in Agribusiness & Applied Economics at North Dakota State University, and I bring eight years of prior experience in banking and corporate finance.

Currently

I'm collaborating with Dr. Jason Grant and Emre Karagulle on a project examining sanitary and phytosanitary (SPS) measures and technical barriers to trade (TBT) affecting U.S. agricultural exports. We are extending an existing database of barriers identified in USTR's National Trade Estimate reports beyond 2021 and exploring how exports adjust over time following the introduction and removal of these measures, using modern event-study and staggered difference-in-differences approaches to study exporter adaptation and post-barrier recovery. Separately, a paper on U.S. tariffs and free trade agreements, co-authored with Chowdhury and Dr. Dragan Miljkovic, is under review at The Manchester School. Full details are on the Research page.
