Manoj Datta

Ph.D. Student in Agricultural and Applied Economics

I study international trade policy and agricultural trade as a Ph.D. student at Virginia Tech, advised by Dr. Jason Grant. My work combines applied econometrics — gravity models, panel data methods, and large-scale price-data construction — with eight years of prior experience in banking and corporate finance. Before Virginia Tech, I earned an M.S. in Agribusiness & Applied Economics at North Dakota State University.

Currently

I'm building a dissertation chapter that constructs a national panel of daily spot prices for corn, soybeans, and wheat at roughly 3,000 U.S. grain elevators — more than 20 million observations spanning 2004–2025 — under the supervision of Dr. Jason Grant. In parallel, a paper on U.S. tariffs and free trade agreements, co-authored with Chowdhury and Dr. Dragan Miljkovic, is under review at The Manchester School. Full details are on the Research page.
